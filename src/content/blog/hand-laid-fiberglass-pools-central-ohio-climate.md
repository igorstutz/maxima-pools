## The Science of Hand-Laid Fiberglass vs. The Central Ohio Freeze

To understand why a San Juan fiberglass pool is superior, you have to look at how it is made. Most manufacturers use a "chopper gun" to spray a mixture of short, chopped fiberglass strands and resin into a mold. This creates a brittle shell with inconsistent thickness, leaving it highly vulnerable to cracking when the ground freezes and expands.

San Juan Pools does things differently. They utilize a meticulous [100% hand-laid construction process](/how-san-juan-pools-are-made) using massive sheets of woven roving fiberglass and premium marine-grade Vinyl Ester resin.

![San Juan technicians laying woven roving fiberglass sheets by hand](/images/how-its-made/step-glass.webp)

*Woven roving sheets are rolled out and laid by hand, layer by layer — never sprayed from a chopper gun.*

- **Aerospace-Level Strength:** This hand-laid process yields an incredibly dense, ultra-strong structure. It is the same rugged construction method used to build high-performance ocean yachts and aerospace components.
- **Flexibility Under Pressure:** Central Ohio soils — from the clay-heavy ground in Lewis Center and Westerville to the rolling landscapes of Powell and Dublin — expand drastically when frozen. A hand-laid pool shell acts like a structural spring. It possesses the high tensile strength to flex with shifting earth and [frost heaving](/frozen-pools), then pop right back into its original shape without structural damage.
- **No Risk of Osmotic Blistering:** The combination of pure Vinyl Ester resin and hand-applied layers completely seals out moisture, preventing water from penetrating the shell and causing blisters.
